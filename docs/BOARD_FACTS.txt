RUNTIME BASELINE

The baseline was built with ESP-IDF v6.0.2 for the pre-v3 ESP32-P4 path.
Image metadata accepts silicon revision v0.0 through v1.99 and the attached
chip is v1.3. A normal non-forced flash wrote the bootloader partition table
and application, verified every image hash, and reset through RTS.

The captured boot proves two cores, a 360 MHz CPU, 16 MiB DIO flash at 40 MHz,
32 MiB PSRAM at 200 MHz, UART logging on GPIO37 and GPIO38, and BOOT GPIO35
idle high. The firmware reached its idle loop without a reset or crash.

The physical pressed level was not sampled because no person was available to
hold the key. The schematic proves that Key2 closes GPIO35 to ground; a later
manual level diagnostic should still confirm raw level 0 while pressed.

PROMPT 03 CABLE AND POWER ARRANGEMENT

Use a known data-capable Type-C cable from the connector labeled USB to the
host for native FIDO HID traffic. This connector is the direct USB1P1 full
speed path. Do not use the Type-A high speed jumper bank for this baseline.

Keep a second known data-capable Type-C cable from PWR USB TO UART to the host
for CH343 flashing and logs. Windows currently identifies that path as COM7.
Both USB inputs join the board 5 V power network through its power selection
circuit, so do not add another 5 V header source. Enumeration must establish
that the native cable carries data; cable shape alone is not proof.
